# 🤖 HabitBot v5.0: Your AI-Powered Mastery Workstation

HabitBot is not just a habit tracker—it is a secure, multi-user, multimodal AI ecosystem designed to turn discipline into an automated lifestyle. Built with a "Mobile-First" philosophy, HabitBot combines the rigor of atomic habit tracking with the intelligence of a personal Llama-powered mastery coach.

## 🚀 Key Features

### 🧠 Multimodal AI Habit Coach
- **Context-Aware Coaching**: The AI analyzes your weekly habit logs, focus sessions, and reflections to provide deeply personalized advice.
- **Vision Integration**: Upload photos of your workspace or handwritten journals, and the coach will analyze them to optimize your environment.
- **Document Intelligence**: Upload PDFs of books or articles, and your coach will help you extract atomic habits from them.

### 📈 Advanced Analytics & Visualization
- **GitHub-Style Heatmap**: A 365-day consistency grid with intensity scaling.
- **Deep Work Engine**: Integrated Pomodoro timer with OS-level notifications and automatic data logging.
- **AI Performance Audits**: Weekly mastery reports generated by AI to identify your "friction points."

### 🔐 Enterprise-Grade Security
- **Multi-User Isolation**: Each user has a completely private database footprint.
- **Persistent Sessions**: Cookie-based "Remember Me" functionality for a seamless native-app feel.
- **Industrial Hashing**: Passwords protected by `bcrypt` (Salted & Hashed).

### 📥 Data Portability
- **Life Audit Export**: Download your entire digital legacy (Habits, Focus, Tasks, Reflections, and Chat History) into a professionally formatted multi-sheet Excel file.

## 🛠 Tech Stack
- **Frontend**: Streamlit (Glassmorphism & PWA Optimized)
- **Intelligence**: Groq / Llama 3.2 (Standard & Vision)
- **Database**: SQLite (Relational & Normalized)
- **Auth**: Bcrypt & Browser Cookies
- **Visuals**: Plotly Express & Custom CSS

## 📲 Installation (Mobile PWA)
HabitBot is optimized for iOS and Android:
1. Open the live URL in your mobile browser.
2. Select **"Add to Home Screen"**.
3. Launch HabitBot from your app drawer for a full-screen, native experience.

## 🛠 Local Setup
1. Clone the repository.
2. Install dependencies: `pip install -r requirements.txt`
3. Add your `GROQ_API_KEY` to a `.env` file.
4. Run: `streamlit run app.py`
